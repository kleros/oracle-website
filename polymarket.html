Why does Polymarket need Kleros?

Because you can't tell which market will blow up.

An optimistic oracle handles the vast majority of markets perfectly well. The ones that turn into angry threads, manual overrides and headlines cannot be picked out in advance. So the answer is a deeper layer behind all of them.

And if the losing side disagrees?

There is no appeal.

So the appeal happens somewhere else. The Strategy bitcoin market is now before the New York Supreme Court, where it costs more, takes years, and is decided by a judge with no knowledge of the market. A resolution layer without an appeal does not remove the appeal. It moves it to a courtroom.

Supreme Court of the State of New York · Index No. 158389/2026

What does Kleros do instead?

It runs a court.

  • 01A panel drawn at random for that one case, unknown until it is drawn.
  • 02Evidence from both sides, and each juror's written justification on a public record.
  • 03An appeal that redraws a larger panel.

A court that doubles every appeal.

What do regulators want?

Due process.

  • 01Someone impartial to decide it.
  • 02Both sides put in evidence.
  • 03A written answer that is final.
The CFTC's own description of an acceptable dispute resolution mechanism
Its words: an “objective and impartial decisionmaker,” an opportunity to be heard, and a “prompt, written, final” award.
A statement that a contract will settle based upon a consensus of yet-to-be-determined sources may not be sufficient.
CFTC Staff Letter No. 26-08 · footnote 26

Sources and methods have to be named and assessed before a contract is listed. No rule has been written yet. That is the useful part.
