Our answer is up within minutes of the whistle. Everything after that is the public challenge window, and its length is a setting we agree with you. A shorter window only means less time for anyone to contest.

You keep the key.

  • 01Before kickoff we publish a question for every market you plan to list.
  • 02You reference it as the market's official source and deploy as you do now.
  • 03After the game your bot reads the result from a public API and you settle.

We never touch your stake, so there is nothing to unwind if you stop. If you would rather not run the settle step at all, you can grant us settle-only permission for these markets and revoke it in one transaction.

We can write the rules too.

  • 01Market textWhat counts, which source decides, and the deadline, drafted before the market lists.
  • 02A resolution policyWhat a tie, a postponed game, a stat correction or an early answer resolves to, written down once for every market.
  • 03The same reading on every marketIf a result is ever disputed, jurors apply rules you signed off before kickoff.
